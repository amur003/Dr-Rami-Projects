How signals work · data source

Signals come from a fixed technical rule set (EMA crossover in the direction of the EMA-200 trend, RSI momentum filter, ATR-based stop and target), evaluated on closed candles; entries fill at the next candle's open. When a candle touches both stop and target, the stop is counted. Past results do not predict future results, and this is not financial advice.

Live data is PAXG/USDT, a gold-backed token that tracks spot gold closely but not exactly. For exact XAUUSD prices, import your broker's 1-minute export.
